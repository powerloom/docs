# Reward Claims

:::info Status
🟠 Claims available now via [mint.powerloom.network](https://mint.powerloom.network)
:::

## Current Status

Reward claims are available now and will remain available until [mint.powerloom.network](https://mint.powerloom.network) goes offline on **July 16, 2026 at 6:00 AM UTC**.

There is no separate claim window. Claims have always been available on the dashboard and continue to be.

## What this means

- All rewards earned up to the [epoch release cutoff](/wind-down/snapshotter-rewards) are claimable.
- You can claim at any time before the dashboard goes offline on July 16, 2026.
- After July 16, 2026, the dashboard will be shut down and claims will no longer be possible through the UI.

## How to claim

1. Visit [mint.powerloom.network](https://mint.powerloom.network).
2. Connect the wallet associated with your node.
3. Navigate to your pending rewards.
4. Submit the claim transaction.
5. Bridge your claimed POWER out of the Powerloom chain via [bridge-v2.powerloom.network](https://bridge-v2.powerloom.network).

:::warning
The Powerloom chain shuts down on **July 21, 2026 at 6:00 AM UTC**. Bridge your tokens out of the chain before then.
:::

## Deadline

**July 16, 2026 at 6:00 AM UTC**. This is when [mint.powerloom.network](/wind-down/dashboards) goes offline. Claim before then.
