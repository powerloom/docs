# Staked POWER

:::info Status
🟠 Unstaking available now via [mint.powerloom.network/staking](https://mint.powerloom.network/staking)
:::

## Current Status

You can unstake your POWER right now at [mint.powerloom.network/staking](https://mint.powerloom.network/staking). There is no cooldown. After you unstake, your withdrawal (claim) of the unstaked POWER is immediate.

For claiming pending rewards, use the main [mint.powerloom.network](https://mint.powerloom.network) dashboard. See [Reward Claims](/wind-down/reward-claims).

## How to unstake and withdraw

1. Visit [mint.powerloom.network/staking](https://mint.powerloom.network/staking).
2. Connect the wallet you staked with.
3. Unstake your POWER and withdraw (claim) it. This is immediate, with no cooldown.
4. Claim any accumulated staking rewards.
5. Bridge your POWER out of the Powerloom chain via [bridge-v2.powerloom.network](https://bridge-v2.powerloom.network).

:::tip Action required

**Unstake, withdraw, and bridge your POWER before the deadlines below.**

- [mint.powerloom.network/staking](https://mint.powerloom.network/staking) goes offline **July 16, 2026 at 6:00 AM UTC**.
- The Powerloom chain shuts down **July 21, 2026 at 6:00 AM UTC**. [Bridge](https://bridge-v2.powerloom.network) your POWER out of the Powerloom chain before then.
:::

See also: [Node Management](/wind-down/node-onboarding) for node burn (no cooldown), and [Reward Claims](/wind-down/reward-claims) for claiming pending snapshotter rewards.
